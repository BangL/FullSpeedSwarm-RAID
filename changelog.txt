Version 0.1 (RAID port of Full Speed Swarm r337 by TdlQ, ported by BangL):
- AI task queue: configurable throughput (RAID's own rate is 80 to 100 tasks per second), executed tasks are cleaned up in one pass, a task queued again before it ran is updated instead of queued twice
- important enemies and snipers are updated every 0.1 s instead of every frame, bots' brains 10 times per second through the task queue
- walking: cached walk direction and rotation, path lengths cached, ground ray reused for a few frames, walking quality option (lod stage per character)
- movement: blocking actions are counted so chk_action_forbidden is skipped when there are none (parachuting enemies still checked), nav segment tracking of enemies
- LOD updater (option), ragdolls of corpses thrown by explosions (option "High violence mode")
- area triggers: shapes check a bounding sphere first, instigator lists are shared between triggers within a frame, spheres and unit shapes supported
- mission callbacks: binary insertion in CallbackHandler, compatible with the callback queue of Field Repairs
- portals, sound environment, controllers, attention settings (cached per handler, dropped when team relations change), areas per nav segment (cache dropped when worlds are merged or unloaded), weapon stats
- the loud state follows the game (EnemyManager:set_hot_state), so camp and stealth missions after a loud one are quiet again (RAID keeps the Lua state between camp and missions)
- gameplay options kept: improved tactics, improved chase (done by FSS through RAID's own path_ahead, in PAYDAY 2 Iter does it), improved tactics (needs Iter RAID), nervous game, enemy awareness, fast-paced game, eyes wide open
- options menu in RAID style (BLT options), english and german
- fixed: ShapeBoxMiddleBottom used an undefined variable, TextGui removed an undefined panel, sound environment objects added later were never updated (typo in the id field)
- not ported, RAID's code differs too much or does it already: detection (RAID's stealth relies on it), enemy and bot target priority, assault tactics (no tactics maps in RAID), filtered attention lists, spawn delay (RAID has _chk_spawn_point_camped), player states and input rewrite, camera, HUD, interactions, vehicles, weapon method caching, coroutines, everything about hostages, tasers, cloakers, sentries, mutators, crime spree, ECM, achievements and other PAYDAY 2 content
- removed options: hostage situation, spawn delay, mutators (Real Elastic, Stealthroids, Big Party)
